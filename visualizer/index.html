Theoretical Complexity Proof & Symmetry Principle

Standard center expansion incurs O(N2) worst-case time (e.g., bitstreams of identical zeros 00000000). Manacher's Algorithm maintains a center C and its rightmost boundary R = C + P[C]. For any position i < R, the mirror index i' = 2C - i provides an immediate lower bound P[i] = min(R - i, P[i']). The right boundary R advances at most 2N times over the entire execution, guaranteeing a strict O(N) upper bound.
